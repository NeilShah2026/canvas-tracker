Import from Canvas

One click — everything

  1. Drag this button up to your bookmarks bar:
    Sync my Canvas
  2. Open Canvas and sign in as normal.
  3. Click the bookmark. Your tracker opens with everything filled in.

Pulls courses, assignments, due dates and prep pages — the readings, videos and problem sets owed before each class. It runs on Canvas's own page using your existing login, so there's no password or access token to hand over, and your assignments travel in the link itself rather than through any server.

Or import a calendar file

  1. In Canvas, open Calendar and click Calendar Feed in the right sidebar.
  2. Open that link in a new tab — your browser downloads a .ics file.
  3. Drop the file below.
